How the app is put together

Nine things happen between your thumb hitting the keys and the words appearing. Every one of them is built and working today.

Keys heldWatched at the Windows level, so it works in any app
→
Bar appearsAlready recording — the half-second before you pressed is kept, so no clipped first word
→
Keys releasedAudio shrunk locally before sending — a third the size
Sent to GroqTranscribed, then optionally rewritten to your tone
→
Clipboard stagedFlagged so Windows won't sync or remember it
→
Pasted & restoredWaits for proof the paste landed, then puts your clipboard back
The one that mattered most. The old version guessed — it waited 550 milliseconds and hoped the paste had happened before restoring your clipboard. This one waits for Windows to confirm the paste actually occurred. That's the difference between "usually works" and "works".
